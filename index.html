Despite Pakistan's constitution AND explicit Islamic Sharia mandates guaranteeing women's inheritance rights, the system is rigged against them. Corrupt village revenue officers (Patwaris) collude with male heirs to erase women from property records.

How the Fraud Works

  1. A father dies, leaving land and property behind
  2. Male heirs (brothers, uncles) approach the local Patwari (village revenue officer)
  3. The Patwari omits daughters from the handwritten family tree (Shajra Nasab)
  4. Or they fabricate a fake "voluntary gift" document (Hiba) claiming the woman "gave up" her share
  5. Civil courts take 15–30 YEARS to resolve — most women give up
  6. Result: 97% of women lose everything. Permanently.
The Islamic Irony

The Quran (4:11, 4:12, 4:176) explicitly mandates precise mathematical shares for female heirs. The very religion invoked to deny women their rights actually guarantees them. HaqDar enforces what the Quran commands.

The Solution

HaqDar is a multi-agent AI platform where 8 specialized agents coordinate in real-time to investigate inheritance fraud, compute legally-mandated shares, detect document tampering, and generate actionable legal recovery roadmaps — all in minutes instead of decades.

Accuracy (Math-Based)
Not a ChatGPT Wrapper

HaqDar doesn't "guess" inheritance shares. The Sharia Faraizi calculation uses deterministic mathematical formulas from the Quran. The AI agents orchestrate, investigate, and validate — the math is provably correct.

Faraizi Calculation Example

03

The 8 Agents

🎯 Case Orchestrator
Supervisor + Router

Central coordinator. Classifies case type, delegates to specialized agents, monitors progress, requests human approval at critical points.

📋 Intake Agent
Sequential

Conducts structured interviews with claimants in Urdu or English. Gathers deceased's info, property details, known heirs.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family Tree Agent
ReAct / Tool-Use

Constructs the complete genealogical tree. Identifies ALL legal heirs — including those deliberately omitted by corrupt officials.

📄 Document Analyzer
Parallel

Processes property documents, registry extracts, gift deeds. Flags suspicious Hiba documents and irregular mutation dates.

⚖️ Sharia Calculator
Sequential + Math Tools

Computes EXACT fractional shares for every heir using Faraizi formulas from the Quran. Deterministic — not hallucinated.

🔍 Fraud Detection
Adversarial / Debate

Cross-references claimed vs. legal distribution. Detects omitted heirs, fake Hiba documents, coerced signatures.

📜 Legal Strategy
Planning + RAG

Creates step-by-step legal recovery roadmap. Recommends Ombudsperson filing, civil court, or mediation based on case specifics.

🛡️ QA Reviewer
Reflection / Self-Correction

Final quality gate. Reviews math accuracy, checks for missed heirs, validates legal citations. Loops back if errors found.
